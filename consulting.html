Consulting

I help research teams and organizations turn questions about beliefs, language, and media into studies and computational tools. My consulting combines language modeling, behavioral research, and data visualization, from studying historical religious texts to understanding how scholars communicate with the public. To discuss a project, use the form below or email j.priniski@gmail.com.

Selected Consulting Projects

Duke Suffering, Compassion, and Human Flourishing Lab

The lab’s examines how beliefs about suffering relate to compassion and a sense of shared humanity.

As a consultant, I will apply computational language analysis to historical texts to understand beliefs about suffering across major religious traditions. This work will compare how those texts explain suffering, its causes, and its meaning, contributing to the team’s broader research on how people understand hardship.

PublicScholarship.org

PublicScholarship.org brings together essays, podcasts, and videos created by scholars for general audiences, making expert knowledge easier to find beyond academic publications.

As an advisor and consultant, I apply language modeling and computational research to understand public scholarship: the ideas scholars share, how they frame those ideas, and how their language varies across topics and disciplines.
