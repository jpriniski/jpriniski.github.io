Hunter Priniski, PhD

Research scientist inspiring human narratives

Postdoctoral Fellow, UCLA

Google Scholar GitHub Email

Emerging technologies provide people with access to vast sources of information while enabling unprecedented levels of social connectivity. Yet the complex dynamics linking individual and group psychology make it difficult to predict how technology impacts people’s thinking and behavior. My experimental research on narratives revolves around two questions: how do emerging technologies affect how people learn, communicate, and shape complex narratives? How can findings inform digital tools and new media that help people shape their lives and communities? My experiments on human groups show how network connections shape which narrative details people emphasize and how information flows through groups. I’m also exploring how AI agents in these networks affect people’s understanding of events and perceptions of shared beliefs. These findings guide development of digital technologies and narrative-based media designed to inspire new forms of human creativity and social connection.

Core Themes

Narrative Agency

How people learn, communicate, and shape the narratives guiding their lives, and how digital tools expand or constrain that ability. Network structure shapes which perspectives become shared.

Explore →

Human–AI Group Alignment

How AI agents’ goals, perspectives, and messages influence human groups, and how to design systems that support shared understanding. Preliminary findings show that concentrated AI signals can steer shared language.

Explore →

Narrative Agency in Practice

Applying research through new media development, from games to community-led AI. I’m developing Digital Community Gardens to help residents share stories, find common concerns, and organize around local technology decisions.
